GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with our team.

What technologies do you use for web development?

We specialize in modern frontend engineering utilizing HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), React, Tailwind CSS, Bootstrap, GitHub workflow, and hosting deployments on Vercel, Netlify, or Pantheon.

Will my website be mobile-responsive and fast?

Absolute requirement! Every site we craft is engineered mobile-first to render perfectly across iPhones, iPads, and high-res desktop monitors, achieving top Google PageSpeed score performance.

How long does it take to complete a project?

Landing pages are usually delivered within 3 to 4 business days. Full multi-page business websites or custom web applications take between 1 to 2 weeks.

Do you offer post-launch maintenance and support?

Yes, all custom development packages include free post-launch support and bug fixes for up to 30 days. Extended maintenance packages are also available.
